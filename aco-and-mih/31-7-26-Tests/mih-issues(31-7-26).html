MIH Transcription Issues Report

Detailed Analysis of Transcription Model Performance — 31/7/26

Executive Summary

On the 31/7/26 MIH test run, WER was elevated across mixed, English, and Urdu samples. The main failure mode observed was large stretches of audio that were not transcribed at all, leaving many ground-truth sentences blank or reduced to a few incorrect words.

Identified Issues

1. The System Mostly Failing to Transcribe Many Sentences

Across multiple samples, the machine transcription column is empty for long stretches while the ground truth contains full sentences. In other rows, only one or two words appear against a complete spoken line. This pattern showed up in mixed, English, and Urdu reports (for example mix-s3, eng-s5, ur-s3, and ur-s10).

  • Many consecutive rows have no machine output at all.
  • Partial rows often keep only a fragment (e.g. a single word) while the rest of the sentence is deleted.
  • This drives the high WER on the 31/7/26 MIH column.

2. System Stops and Ends the Meeting on Its Own

Sometimes the system stops and ends the meeting in the middle of an active session on its own, before the meeting has actually finished.

Gentle Note
