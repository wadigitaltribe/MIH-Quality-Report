Issues

  1. Substitution Errors: High substitution rates occur due to homophones (e.g., "problem" → "مسائل") and incorrect word mappings (e.g., "unemployment" → "چاہتا").
  2. Insertion Runs: Long, unsegmented strings of text are inserted, often mixing languages (e.g., "اور یہ Pakistan Many young people...").
  3. Deletions: Specific words are missing entirely, particularly English articles ("In", "the") and Urdu particles ("[بے]").

Suggestions

  1. Audio Preprocessing: Apply noise reduction and spectral enhancement to improve clarity, as audio artifacts often cause substitution errors.
  2. Language Model Tuning: Adjust the language model weights to better recognize the specific Urdu-English code-switching patterns present in the dataset.
  3. Vocabulary Expansion: Manually add domain-specific vocabulary (e.g., "entrepreneurship", "education") to the ASR vocabulary to prevent hallucinations.
  4. Post-Processing Rules: Implement a rule-based post-processor to insert missing English articles ("the", "a") and Urdu particles ("بے") that are frequently deleted.
  5. Punctuation Normalization: Configure the ASR to output punctuation consistently to reduce the likelihood of insertion runs.
