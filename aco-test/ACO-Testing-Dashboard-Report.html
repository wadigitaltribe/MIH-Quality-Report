ACO Testing Observations

4 open
OBS-01Critical

Participant transcription not generated

In multi-participant conversations, transcription is generated only for the person who creates the meeting (e.g., Secretary), but is missing for other participants. Transcription should be available for every participant in the meeting.

https://aco.digitaltribe.cloud/meetings/669a6e14-fa99-4407-b6fa-1fc0fc53b20b
OBS-02High

Duplicate transcription entries

Some dialogue lines are displayed more than once during Urdu transcription, producing duplicate entries in the transcript. The same lines transcribe correctly, without duplication, in English.

Urdu — duplicates lines English — correct
OBS-03Medium

Repeated "Thank you" message

The system repeatedly inserts a "Thank you" message multiple times during transcription, even when it was spoken only once, or was not part of the conversation at all.

OBS-04Critical

Missing transcription segments

Certain portions of the conversation are not transcribed at all, indicating that parts of the audio are being missed by the transcription engine. This results in incomplete transcripts.
