Issues found in the machine transcription

  • [Manual override] 22 alignment op(s) reclassified by you (local rescore, no AI).
  • Insertions dominate: 51 inserted words (31% of all errors). Without insertions the WER would be 17.87% instead of 26.09%.
  • The ASR appends its own English translations/paraphrases of Urdu sentences instead of only transcribing. Examples: "speech to text engine".
  • 35 true substitutions (phonetic mishears), e.g. 'ka' -> 'کی', 'objective' -> 'اورڈیکٹیو', 'Hub' -> 'ہپ', 'quality' -> 'بھی'.
  • 76 deletions (words missing from the machine output): 'ka', 'ne', 'Hamza:', 'conversation', 'flow', 'ke'.

Suggestions to reduce WER

  • Disable translation behaviour: set the ASR task to 'transcribe' (not 'translate'), or instruct the model to output verbatim speech only, in the original language mix.
  • Post-process: drop sentences that duplicate the meaning of the immediately preceding segment in another language (near-duplicate detection before scoring or delivery).
  • Improve acoustic robustness for mishears: cleaner audio (less noise, better mic), a domain lexicon / hotword list with the expected vocabulary, and a spelling-correction post-processing pass for known confusion pairs.
  • Check for audio dropouts / aggressive VAD trimming causing missed words; lower the VAD threshold or use overlapping chunks.

AI Analysis (GLM)

Issues

  1. Semantic Substitutions: Frequent misinterpretations of context, such as "lekin" (but) transcribed as "abhi" (now), "integration" as "testing", and "to" as "mujhe" (to me). Other errors include "quality" → "bhi" (also), "aur" (and) → "hum" (we), and "complete" → "that".
  2. Content Word Deletions: A high deletion rate (75) is removing key technical terms like "backend", "end-to-end", "engine", "deadline", and "context".
  3. Hallucinated Insertions: Long, repetitive Urdu phrases (e.g., "اگر آپ کو ایسی طرح پہنچنے کے لئے...") and the phrase "speech to text engine" are being inserted, disrupting the transcript flow.

Suggestions

  1. Acoustic Tuning: Increase sensitivity for function words (prepositions, conjunctions) to reduce the high rate of deletions (e.g., "ka", "ne", "ke").
  2. Language Model Optimization: Adjust LM weights to better distinguish between similar-sounding Urdu/English words and improve handling of code-switching boundaries.
  3. Post-Processing: Implement a repetition filter to remove the long insertion runs observed in the error samples.
