Transcript Cross-Pollination Across Concurrent Meetings

DEFECT-03 Critical
Meeting Stability and Termination
Area: Session stability · Dashboard status sync
Issue
Meetings are terminating prematurely, often within two minutes of initiation.
Observation
There is a discrepancy in the dashboard status, where a meeting is displayed as "In Progress", but upon opening, the status reflects as "Ended."
