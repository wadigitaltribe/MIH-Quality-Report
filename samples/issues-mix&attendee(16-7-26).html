MIH Transcription Issues Report

Issues Observed During Mix & Attendee Transcription Testing — 16/7/26

Executive Summary

During mix and attendee transcription testing, four recurring issues were observed. Each issue is documented below with supporting screenshots. Use the left/right controls on each slider to review the evidence for that issue.

Identified Issues

Issue 1

Repeats the Same Sentence When It Was Not Spoken

Sometimes the model repeats the same sentence even when that content was not spoken by the person.

Issue 2

Gets Stuck Repeating the Same Line

Sometimes the model keeps transcribing the same line again and again — as if it is stuck — and does not move on to the next spoken content.

Issue 3

Translates Urdu Sentences into English

Instead of transcribing Urdu speech as spoken, the model sometimes translates Urdu sentences into English.

Issue 4

Misses One or More Sentences

Sometimes the model skips transcription for one or more spoken sentences entirely.
