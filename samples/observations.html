MIH Transcription Observations Report

Detailed Analysis of Transcription Model Performance

Executive Summary

The transcription model demonstrates inconsistency across different sessions. While it transcribes accurately in many cases, it frequently encounters the issues outlined below.

Identified Issues

1. Translates Urdu-English Mix Audio Instead of Pure Transcription

  • e.g.: لیکن صرف سپیچز سے ملک چینج نہیں ہوتا (ground truth) → "But only speeches do not change the country."
  • e.g.: "we have a team of 23 people" (ground truth) → "۔ ہماری ٹیم 23 لوگ ہیں"
  • e.g.: "So how do you keep yourself grounded is" (ground truth) → "سو ہاؤ ڈو یو کیف یورسل گراؤنڈڈ ایز"

2. Model Gets Stuck with Repetitions

The model sometimes repeats the same sentences 2 or 3 times.

3. Misses Entire Sentences

The model occasionally skips entire sentences in the transcription.

4. Audio Recorded but No Transcription Generated

https://mih.pda.gov.pk/meetings/55ab0194-fecd-414f-8ba1-904261424501

5. Skipped, Repeated, and Fabricated Content

Skipped lines, repeated content, and added made-up lines not present in the original recording.

https://mih.pda.gov.pk/meetings/903875f9-e07b-4952-9dfc-2c7d47c2783f

6. Extra Incorrect Lines

In Urdu and mixed samples, the system adds lines even when this was not spoken in the recording.

e.g.: اردو اور English ملا کر بولی گفتگو ہے

7. Cross-Meeting Transcription Leakage

Two meetings were running side by side. The 1st meeting's transcription was shown in the 2nd meeting's transcription. However, when the 2nd meeting ended, that extra transcription was removed automatically.

8. Duplicate Output with Translation

In some cases, the system writes the original line and also adds its translated version, which creates unnecessary duplication.
